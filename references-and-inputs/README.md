# References and Inputs

This folder gathers the source material consulted while preparing the Track 1 Issuer Onboarding and Governance working draft.

- [Nair's India architecture comparison, September 24, 2026](nair-india-architecture-comparison-2026-09-24.md) — additional internal co-lead preparation against current scope; not a source submitted to or adopted by the task force.

The material is organized into three groups:

- `captures/` contains Markdown captures and summaries of resources shared through the GDC Trust Registries Task Force email thread, especially the thread titled `REMINDER: Trust Registries Meeting Today @ 16:30 CET`, shared through the TR-TF Google Group between June 10 and June 15, 2026.
- `originals/` contains original files that were available locally, including the SIDI Hub trust-management Word documents and the eduGAIN OpenID Federation presentation.
- `ayra-context/` contains Ayra-generated synthesis and working notes that materially shaped the draft. These are not external GDC source materials, but they are important context for how the draft was consolidated and framed.

The draft itself should treat the externally shared resources and Ayra-context materials differently. The external resources are prior art shared with the group. The Ayra-context files explain how Ayra interpreted that prior art, where Ayra sees gaps, and how the scope moved from the earlier RP/verifier framing toward issuer onboarding and governance.

Two structural changes have since overtaken parts of this folder, and older files still carry the earlier framing:

- **The work moved from RP/verifier onboarding to issuer onboarding and governance.** Files named for RP onboarding record the earlier scope.
- **Track 3 was folded into Track 1; there is no Track 3.** The task force runs two working tracks — Track 1 (Policy, Taxonomy & Scope; Onboarding & Governance) and Track 2 (Architecture & Technical). Darrell is a Track 1 co-lead with Apple and France. Files named `track3-*` are retained under their original names to keep existing links intact.

Some copied Markdown captures preserve Obsidian wikilinks from the Ayra knowledge base. In this repository, use the folder structure above as the source of truth: external captures are in `captures/`, and Ayra interpretation/context is in `ayra-context/`.
