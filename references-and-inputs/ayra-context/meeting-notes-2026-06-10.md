

What we have learned, at Ayra, about Trust Registries is that the utility and authority of a trust registry is in the eye of the beholder. 

Different parties (RPs) have very different needs - and thus need answers to very different questions:
- What authorization am I checking? 
- What gives a trust registry any grounding in authority?

Question Buckets:
- Is an Issuer (IdP) authorized?
	- ... to (issue) a particular type of credential (resource) under a particular governance scheme?
- Is a wallet approved?
- Is a verifier (RP) authorized?
	- ... to (request) a (presentation) of a credential?
