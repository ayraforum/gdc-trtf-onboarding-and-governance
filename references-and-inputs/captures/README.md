# GDC Trust Registries Task Force — Reference Knowledge Base

This folder captures the **key documents shared in the GDC Trust Registries Task Force (TR-TF) mailing list thread** ("REMINDER: Trust Registries Meeting Today @ 16:30 CET", June 10–15 2026), plus an Ayra-specific synthesis.

These are **external documents authored by others** (SIDI Hub / ITU-T editors, W3C, OpenID Foundation, GDC Secretariat, UN/CEFACT). They are captured here for internal study and to inform Ayra's participation in the TR-TF. Ayra's own positions live elsewhere in the vault — see [[SYNTHESIS]] for where they align and diverge.

## Context

The [Global Digital Collaboration (GDC)](https://globaldigitalcollaboration.org/) stood up a **Trust Registries Task Force**, co-led by **France (ANTS, Ministry of the Interior)** and **Apple**. Ayra (Darrell O'Donnell) is a member and has volunteered as a **champion for Track 3: RP Onboarding & Governance**. Documents were shared into the task force to "baseline current work" and serve as prior art.
